About

RAJURESHWAR JEWELLERS

Established in 1940 by Kisanrao Ambadas Jade, Rahureshwar Jewellers has grown into a trusted name in the jewellery industry. With a legacy of purity and transparency, our brand has been built on strong customer trust and excellence in craftsmanship. Under the leadership of Ashok Kisan Jade, we have expanded our presence and continue to offer the finest gold and silver jewellery.

For over eight decades, we have been committed to delivering intricate designs, superior quality, and unmatched service. Our dedication to traditional artistry, combined with modern innovation, allows us to create timeless jewellery pieces that celebrate every special moment. Whether it’s a wedding, festival, or a personal milestone, Rahureshwar Jewellers ensures that every piece tells a story of elegance and trust.
