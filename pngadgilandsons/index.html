About Us

P N Gadgil & Sons

With the legacy of six generations, P N Gadgil & Sons has become one of Maharashtra's most trusted and reputed jewellery brands, built on trust, transparency, craftsmanship and warm customer relationships.

Since 1832

Six Generations of Excellence

1832

The Beginning

Founded in Sangli by Ganesh Narayan Gadgil, P N Gadgil began its journey with a commitment to quality, craftsmanship and trust, laying the foundation for one of India's most respected jewellery brands.

1958

Expansion to Pune

The fourth generation expanded the business to Pune, introducing the brand to a wider audience and establishing a legacy of superior service and transparency.

Today

A Legacy of Excellence

With over 1.5 million satisfied customers across 25 cities and 33 showrooms in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Gujarat, P N Gadgil & Sons continues to lead with innovation, timeless designs and unmatched customer service.
