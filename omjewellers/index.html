About Us

Tradition, Trust & Timeless Craftsmanship

Om Jewellers is a BIS certified Indian jewellery group in Balinge established in 1991. Started with 916 hallmark jewellery, offering exclusive varieties of every item at affordable prices.

Shop for giftable gold items, high-quality, best-value jewellery for Indian women. Easy exchange, purity guaranteed, exclusive designs, and affordable pricing.

Our customers are satisfied with our best quality and staff service. We also provide jewellery insurance on items.

Om Jewellers has the latest designer and traditional collection of gold, silver, and diamond jewellery, including antique varieties.

We offer opportunities to invest through monthly gold saving schemes. Our mission is to design jewellery that is affordable to everyone and loved by consumers.
