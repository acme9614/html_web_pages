Welcome to Mahendra Jewellers

Founded In 1907, MAHENDRA JEWELLERS Is One Of The Oldest And Most Reputed Jewellers In The Country. It’s A Legacy Of Over Four Generations Which Is Built On Trust, Excellence And On Warm Relationship With Customers And Society.

Mahendra Jewellers was the first hallmarked gold jeweller, the first ISO 9001 gold jeweller and the first IGI certified diamond jeweller in Southern Maharashtra. In 2005, it received India’s first hallmarking for silver jewellery.
