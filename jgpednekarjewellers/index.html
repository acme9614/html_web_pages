Know More About Us:

Welcome to JEWELLERS JAGANNATH GANGARAM PEDNEKAR — where tradition meets timeless elegance. It's a name that generations have trusted for quality, purity, and craftsmanship.

Rooted in the cultural heart of Maharashtra, we curate jewellery that reflects our rich heritage and modern sophistication. From exquisite Maharashtrian nath to royal bridal sets — every piece is handcrafted with love and precision.

With our new App and website, customers can now order from home. We offer a wide variety of designs and provide hallmark jewellery of the highest quality.
